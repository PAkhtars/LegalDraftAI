AFFIDAVIT
I, Rajesh Kumar Sharma, S/o Late Sh. Dayanand, aged about 45 years, residing at Chandni Chowk, Delhi, do hereby solemnly affirm and state on oath as under:
1. That I am the plaintiff/applicant in the above‑captioned suit and am fully conversant with the facts and circumstances of the case and therefore competent to depose to this affidavit.
2. That on 01‑04‑2024 I advanced a friendly loan of Rs.5,00,000 (Rupees Five Lakh only) to the defendant, Suresh Chand, Proprietor of M/s Super Traders, against a promissory note, the terms of which stipulated interest at the rate of 12% per annum and repayment on or before 31‑03‑2025.
3. That the defendant made a part payment of Rs.50,000 on 15‑05‑2025, leaving a net principal outstanding of Rs.4,50,000. Interest accrued thereon at 12% per annum amounts to Rs.60,000, making the total amount claimed Rs.5,10,000.
4. That a legal notice dated 15‑06‑2026 was served on the defendant demanding payment of the outstanding sum of Rs.5,10,000, but the defendant failed to comply with the notice.
5. That I have reason to believe, and have in my possession information indicating, that the defendant is actively attempting to dispose of, transfer or alienate his immovable properties and other assets, as evidenced by proposed sale deed records, with the intention of defeating creditors and evading execution of any decree that may be passed in my favour.
6. That such threatened alienation, if allowed to continue, will cause irreparable injury to my rights and will render any future decree unenforceable, thereby defeating the purpose of this suit.
7. That under Order XXXIX Rules 1‑2 of the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908, I am entitled to seek an interim injunction restraining the defendant from alienating, selling, transferring or otherwise dealing with his immovable properties and assets pending the final disposal of the suit.
8. That the circumstances disclosed above constitute urgency warranting the grant of interim relief, as any delay may result in loss of assets beyond the control of this Hon’ble Court.
9. That the facts stated in paragraphs 1 to 8 are within my personal knowledge and belief and are supported by the promissory note dated 01‑04‑2024, the ledger statements, the amount‑breakup calculation, the legal notice dated 15‑06‑2026 and the proposed sale deed records.
