AFFIDAVIT
I, Rajesh Kumar Sharma, S/o Late Sh. Dayanand, aged about 45 years, residing at Chandni Chowk, Delhi, do hereby solemnly affirm and state on oath as under:
1. That I am the plaintiff in the above‑captioned suit and am fully conversant with the facts and circumstances of the case and therefore competent to depose to this affidavit.
2. That on 01‑04‑2024 I advanced a friendly loan of Rs.5,00,000 (Rupees Five Lakh only) to the defendant, Suresh Chand, Proprietor of M/s Super Traders, against a promissory note, the terms of which stipulated interest at the rate of 12% per annum and repayment on or before 31‑03‑2025.
3. That the defendant made a part payment of Rs.50,000 on 15‑05‑2025, leaving a net principal outstanding of Rs.4,50,000.  Interest accrued thereon at 12% per annum amounts to Rs.60,000, making the total amount claimed Rs.5,10,000.
4. That a legal notice dated 15‑06‑2026 was served on the defendant demanding payment of the outstanding sum of Rs.5,10,000, but the defendant failed to comply with the notice.
5. That as a result of the defendant’s non‑compliance, a cause of action arose and I instituted the present suit for recovery of money on 19‑10‑2026 before the Senior Civil Judge, Tis Hazari, Delhi.
6. That I have reason to believe, and have in my possession information indicating, that the defendant may dispose of, alienate, transfer or otherwise deal with his assets and properties with the intention of defeating or delaying the lawful claim of the plaintiff.
7. That unless appropriate protective orders are passed, there is a serious risk that the assets and properties in question may be wasted, mismanaged or placed beyond the reach of this Hon’ble Court, thereby frustrating the effective enforcement of any decree that may be passed in favour of the plaintiff.
8. That in the facts and circumstances of the case, the appointment of a Receiver under Order XL Rule 1 of the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908, is necessary and just for the preservation, protection and proper management of the property concerned, subject to such terms and conditions as this Hon’ble Court may deem fit.
9. That the facts stated above are within my personal knowledge and belief and are supported by the promissory note dated 01‑04‑2024, the ledger statements, the amount‑breakup calculation, the legal notice dated 15‑06‑2026 and the suit valuation of Rs.5,10,000.
